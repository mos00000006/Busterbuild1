BUSTERBUILD SALES APP — READY TO UPLOAD

1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open your BusterBuild sales WEBSITE repository (not the Company Hub).
3. Upload the included sales-app folder at the repository root and replace the five matching files. The destination must be sales-app/index.html, NOT sales-app/sales-app/index.html. Keep all other existing website files.
4. Commit and let the sales website deploy. No Python installer is needed.
5. Close and reopen the sales app, or use its Update button. The app version is now 2.2.1.

The Hub live-price patch must also be deployed. In Hub > Price tag generator > BusterBuild, find the product, enter the price, keep publishing selected and click Generate. The product code must match the sales catalogue. Open sales apps check once a minute, on return to the app and on reconnect.

The catalogue status shows whether Hub prices were loaded, whether no published prices exist, whether codes do not match, or whether the connection failed. An offline device cannot receive a new price. Saved quotations keep their original price; active carts update.

This patch was applied directly to your uploaded sales-app.zip. It changes the active kitchen script referenced by your live index, activates the price module and refreshes the app/service-worker cache versions. It does not include login settings or replace Firebase configuration, catalogues, pictures or icons. The accidentally nested sales-app/sales-app folder is not used by the main page; this patch does not require it.

Verification: JavaScript syntax checks; active app integration test with your HTML (initial Hub price, open-product update, active cart update and failure warning); five price-sync module tests. Production deployment and live Hub connectivity were not verified here.

BUSTERBUILD HARDWARE — STATIC WEBSITE PACKAGE

Upload the CONTENTS of this ZIP into your website's document root so index.html is at the root. This is a static HTML/CSS/JavaScript site. It needs no database, npm install, or build command. Serve it over HTTPS. The .htaccess file adds basic headers and a 404 page on compatible Apache hosting.

PUBLIC CUSTOMER FLOW
- Catalogue, category pages, product search, cart and saved items work in the browser.
- The quote form opens a prefilled WhatsApp message to +27 63 251 3656. The customer must press Send in WhatsApp. Opening WhatsApp does not submit the enquiry.
- Catalogue totals are indicative. The store confirms current price, stock and delivery charges before payment.
- The site does not collect card details or process payments. Customer accounts and the internal CRM are not part of this public package.
- Cart and saved items persist only in that customer's browser. They are not shared with store staff until the WhatsApp enquiry is sent.

BEFORE PUBLICATION
1. Verify the store's phone (+27 10 101 0464), WhatsApp (+27 63 251 3656), email, address and opening hours.
2. Review the 267 catalogue search entries, their displayed prices, product descriptions and current stock. The supplied content was used; no live stock or price integration exists.
3. Test the WhatsApp destination on a phone and confirm your team will receive and respond to enquiries.
4. Open index.html, search.html, cart.html, checkout.html and contact.html on desktop and mobile under the actual domain.
5. Set DNS and HTTPS in your hosting control panel. This ZIP does not change domain settings or publish itself.

Internal CRM: the supplied CRM contains sample figures and browser-only storage, with no real staff authentication. It was excluded from this public upload. A secure server and database are needed before it can be hosted for staff.

Third-party fonts and icons load from Google Fonts and cdnjs; the pages retain fallback typography if those services are unavailable.

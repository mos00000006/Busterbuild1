BusterBuild Sales App — Welcome Motion Update

Adds a branded opening animation inspired by the prior PowerBuild welcome treatment,
adapted to BusterBuild's black, yellow and white identity.

OPENING MOTION:
WELCOME TO
BUSTERBUILD
TILES & SANITARY SALES
FIND IT. SCAN IT. SELL IT.

Behaviour:
- Runs when the staff Sales App opens.
- Approximately 3 seconds.
- Logo animates in.
- "BUSTERBUILD" reveals across the screen.
- Yellow/white diagonal construction-brand accents animate in.
- Progress line completes, then the Sales App appears.
- Customer shelf/price-tag QR product links skip the animation so customers reach the product immediately.
- Respects reduced-motion accessibility settings.
- Same permanent app URL.
